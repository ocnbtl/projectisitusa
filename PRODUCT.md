# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People exploring their county, learning about invasive species, and checking what the available evidence supports.

## Product Purpose

Make county-level species information understandable, with provenance, observation dates, uncertainty, and useful next steps.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing Next.js application provides a county map, county and ZIP lookup, species profiles, and research views. Preserve routes, aliases, image credits, immutable versioned delivery, and on-demand loading. Include Alaska, Hawaii, DC, and current Connecticut planning regions. Research stays offline. Counts measure documented matching species, never abundance or danger. Missing records do not establish absence. Non-detection, unresolved research, historical occurrence, and current authority status remain distinct.

## Brand Commitments

Project Isitusa. Full-screen map, floating glass controls, minimal and functional information design. Existing project logo retained. Plain, specific language.

## Evidence on Hand

Versioned map and catalog assets; hash-checked published research projections; source citations; curated and registry-only species profiles. Published research and the map snapshot are separate releases.

## Open Decisions

No confirmed payment recipient, subscription delivery/storage, or moderated sightings backend. Do not fabricate destinations or submission success.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable county search, responsive panels, readable contrast, reduced motion, and a non-map route to information.
