# Batch 4 species profile research notes

Reviewed: 2026-10-09

Scope: exact queue orders 151-200. All 50 profiles are present in editorial.json, with source links and reviewedAt. This is reader-facing editorial research, not a distribution audit, taxonomic catalog migration, or management recommendation. Catalog IDs remain unchanged. No repository source, evidence projection, build, deployment, credential, or publication changes were made.

Sources were searched and meaningful relevant pages opened for every species. Some FNA and USGS NAS pages exposed substantive indexed descriptions but failed direct retrieval; the affected entries disclose this and use an additional opened primary or institutional source. All summaries are paraphrases. No source snippets are copied verbatim as profiles. Potential impact is kept distinct from demonstrated impact. General botanical habitat and native-range information do not establish current US local presence.

## Evidence by catalog ID

### acalypha-australis

Iowa State supports annual habit, toothed leaves, copper-colored growth and heart-shaped floral bracts. Native copperleaf confusion matters; no nationwide risk claim made.

- [Iowa State University Extension: Asian copperleaf](https://crops.extension.iastate.edu/encyclopedia/asian-copperleaf)

### ocinebrellus-inornatus

Smithsonian supports shallow oyster-bed habitat, shelled prey and oyster-transfer pathway from the northwest Pacific. Shellfish effects are documented, not inferred from listing alone.

- [Smithsonian NEMESIS: Ocinebrellus inornatus](https://invasions.si.edu/nemesis/species_summary/73246)

### cyperus-microiria

FNA indexed description supports tufted annual habit, triangular stems, V-shaped leaves and spikelet scales; direct retrieval errored. Opened University of Trieste flora supports annual Asian sedge on muddy river and ditch margins.

- [Flora of North America: Cyperus microiria](https://www.efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=200026699)
- [University of Trieste: Cyperus microiria](https://dryades.units.it/dolomitibellunesi/index.php?id=8092&procedure=taxon_page)

### undaria-pinnatifida

Opened Rhode Island CRMC identification PDF supports adult midrib, basal sporophyll, equipment attachment and shading. Its local potential-invader framing is not converted into a Rhode Island occurrence claim.

- [Rhode Island Coastal Resources Management Council: Asian kelp identification card](https://www.crmc.ri.gov/invasives/invasives_refcards/U_pinnatifida.pdf)

### asian-longhorned-beetle

APHIS supports hardwood hosts, internal tunneling, round exit holes, coarse frass and tree weakening. No changing quarantine or regulatory details included.

- [USDA APHIS: Asian longhorned beetle](https://www.aphis.usda.gov/plant-pests-diseases/alb)

### assiminea-parasitologica

Smithsonian supports small brown conical shell and high tidal marsh habitat. Source reports no observed ecological impacts in introduced Oregon populations; potential competition remains explicitly uncertain.

- [Smithsonian NEMESIS: Assiminea parasitologica](https://invasions.si.edu/nemesis/species_summary/-456/1000)

### limnophila-sessiliflora

UF IFAS supports contrasting submerged/emergent leaves, flower colors, fragment regeneration, dense growth, shading and water-use interference.

- [University of Florida IFAS: Limnophila sessiliflora](https://plant-directory.ifas.ufl.edu/plant-directory/limnophila-sessiliflora/)

### brassica-tournefortii

UC IPM supports winter-annual timing, rapid life cycle, desert dunes/washes/shrublands and southwestern North American invasion. No nationwide presence claim.

- [University of California IPM: Sahara mustard](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/natural-area-pests/sahara-mustard/)

### colubrina-asiatica

UF IFAS supports scrambling evergreen habit, glossy toothed leaves, green flowers, capsules and long branches. Warm coastal setting is habitat context, not a local occurrence record.

- [University of Florida IFAS: Colubrina asiatica](https://plant-directory.ifas.ufl.edu/plant-directory/colubrina-asiatica/)

### pachycondyla-chinensis

NC State and Virginia Tech support appearance, damp nesting, termite predation, native-ant displacement and sting. Virginia Tech explicitly maps old Pachycondyla to Brachyponera chinensis. Catalog ID retained.

- [North Carolina State Extension: Asian needle ant](https://entomology.ces.ncsu.edu/asian-needle-ant/)
- [Virginia Tech Extension: Asian needle ant](https://www.pubs.ext.vt.edu/content/dam/pubs_ext_vt_edu/ENTO/ENTO-29/ENTO-554.pdf)

### clitoria-ternatea

NC State Plant Toolbox supports twining habit, conspicuous blue/violet flowers, flattened pods and warm-climate perennial versus annual cultivation. No invasion impact claimed.

- [North Carolina State Extension Plant Toolbox: Clitoria ternatea](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/clitoria-ternatea/)

### dichondra-micrantha

NZ Plant Conservation Network reproduces Flora of New Zealand material on lawn habit, nearly glabrous leaves and taxonomic uncertainty. Similar names may be misapplied; profile avoids strong distribution/impact claims.

- [New Zealand Plant Conservation Network: Dichondra micrantha, drawing on Flora of New Zealand](https://www.nzpcn.org.nz/flora/species/dichondra-micrantha/)

### hemigrapsus-sanguineus

Smithsonian supports square carapace, three lateral teeth, rocky intertidal habitat, flexible diet and effects in invaded Atlantic communities. This does not establish presence at every rocky shore.

- [Smithsonian NEMESIS: Hemigrapsus sanguineus](https://invasions.si.edu/nemesis/species_summary/-2)

### phakopsora-pachyrhizi

NC State supports windborne rust spores, obligate living hosts, kudzu reservoir and impaired seed/pod filling. Profile describes the disease caused by catalog fungus without adding current outbreak claims.

- [North Carolina State Extension: Asian soybean rust](https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/asian-soybean-rust)

### arivela-viscosa

PlantNET opened and FNA indexed descriptions support sticky hairs, yellow flowers, usually five leaflets, narrow capsules and disturbed habitat. Cleome viscosa synonym explained; authorities differ in preferred genus.

- [Royal Botanic Gardens Sydney PlantNET: Arivela viscosa](https://plantnet.rbgsyd.nsw.gov.au/cgi-bin/NSWfl.pl?lvl=sp&name=Arivela~viscosa&page=nswfl)
- [Flora of North America: Arivela viscosa](https://www.efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=250055272)

### monopterus-albus

USGS NAS indexed species account supplies behavior and taxonomic-complex caveat; direct page returned 403. Opened FWS risk PDF corroborates biology. Older risk-report absence-of-impact claims not carried over; no precise US distribution inferred.

- [USGS Nonindigenous Aquatic Species: Asian swamp eel](https://nas.er.usgs.gov/queries/factsheet.aspx?SpeciesID=974)
- [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service: Asian swamp eel risk summary](https://www.fws.gov/sites/default/files/documents/Ecological-Risk-Screening-Summary-Asian-Swamp-Eel.pdf)

### nephrolepis-brownii

USF Florida Invasive Plants supports tall overlapping fronds, blunt pinnae, dark basal scales, shaded well-drained habitat, spores and dense growth. Similar native swordferns require care; older N. multiflora usage exists.

- [University of South Florida: Florida Invasive Plants, Nephrolepis brownii](https://plantatlas.usf.edu/flip/plant.aspx?id=51)

### schyzocotyle-acheilognathi

Opened USGS primary detection-study abstract supports fish hosts and live baitfish pathway. NAS indexed account supports nutrition/growth effects; direct page returned 403. No claim of demonstrated Great Lakes harm.

- [USGS: Asian fish tapeworm detection study](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/loop-mediated-isothermal-amplification-lamp-assay-detection-asian-fish-tapeworm)
- [USGS Nonindigenous Aquatic Species: Asian tapeworm](https://nas.er.usgs.gov/queries/FactSheet.aspx?SpeciesID=2798)

### aedes-albopictus

UF IFAS supports daytime activity, small container breeding and international used-tire pathway. Dated disease-risk statements omitted entirely.

- [University of Florida IFAS: Asian tiger mosquito](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/IN594)

### penaeus-monodon

Smithsonian supports banded appearance, juvenile estuary/adult marine life cycle and aquaculture escape. Competition and disease-transfer concerns remain potential, not proven everywhere; no confirmed breeding claim.

- [Smithsonian NEMESIS: Penaeus monodon](https://invasions.si.edu/nemesis/species_summary/95638)

### elatine-ambigua

FNA indexed and opened IISc herbarium support wet-mud habitat and small aquatic habit. Opened Rosman et al. 2016 primary paper supports recurved fruiting stalks and submerged similarity to E. triandra. ResearchGate hosts the author paper, not an independent tertiary claim.

- [Flora of North America: Elatine ambigua](https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=200014277)
- [Indian Institute of Science Herbarium JCB: Elatine ambigua](https://indiaflora-ces.iisc.ac.in/FloraPeninsular/herbsheet.php?cat=7&id=3567)
- [Rosman and colleagues (2016): New records of Elatine ambigua](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/303980729_New_Records_of_Elatine_ambigua_Elatinaceae_a_Nonindigenous_North_American_Species)

### corbicula-fluminea

Opened Missouri Conservation account supports shell shape/ridges and filter feeding. USGS indexed NAS account notes unresolved Corbicula identities and aggregation under fluminea; direct access errors recorded. No precise range or lineage identification attempted.

- [Missouri Department of Conservation: Asian clam](https://mdc.mo.gov/discover-nature/field-guide/asian-clam)
- [USGS Nonindigenous Aquatic Species: Corbicula fluminea](https://nas.er.usgs.gov/queries/factsheet.aspx?SpeciesID=92)

### commelina-communis

Iowa State supports two conspicuous blue petals plus small white petal, short flower duration, sheathing leaves and possible smartweed confusion.

- [Iowa State University Extension: Asiatic dayflower](https://crops.extension.iastate.edu/encyclopedia/asiatic-dayflower)

### rumex-confertus

Opened APHIS weed-risk PDF supports basal rosette, upright habit, seed/vegetative reproduction and grassland/river/disturbed habitats. Density and shading effects are conditional; risk-assessment uncertainty retained.

- [USDA APHIS: Weed risk assessment for Rumex confertus](https://www.aphis.usda.gov/media/document/34054/file)

### maladera-formosae

University of Maine supports cinnamon coffee-bean adults, nocturnal broad-host feeding and root-feeding grubs. Source explains newer formosae usage for the insect historically called castanea.

- [University of Maine Extension: Asiatic garden beetle](https://extension.umaine.edu/home-and-garden-ipm/fact-sheets/common-name-listing/asiatic-garden-beetle/)

### striga-asiatica

Opened APHIS environmental assessment biology section supports coarse-haired narrow-leaved small plant, grass-root parasitism, crop hosts and tiny seeds. No eradication procedure or legal advice included.

- [USDA APHIS: Witchweed biology and eradication program assessment](https://www.aphis.usda.gov/sites/default/files/witchweed-north-south-carolina.pdf)

### crioceris-asparagi

University of Wisconsin entomology supports dark pale-marked adults, standing rows of eggs, feeding larvae, soil pupation and distinction from orange spotted asparagus beetle.

- [University of Wisconsin Vegetable Entomology: Asparagus beetle](https://vegento.russell.wisc.edu/pests/asparagus-beetle/)

### asparagus-densiflorus

Wisconsin horticulture supports modified-stem foliage, reduced true leaves, flowering/berries and bird dispersal. Horticultural references sometimes combine densiflorus and aethiopicus/Sprengeri; profile uses shared botanical traits, preserves catalog identity and avoids locality/impact claims.

- [University of Wisconsin Extension: Asparagus fern](https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/asparagus-fern-asparagus-densiflorus/)

### phyllonorycter-apparella

PNW university handbook supports tiny adult moth, aspen/cottonwood leaf mines, early sap feeding, later tissue feeding and silk-induced tent/ridge. No nonnative status inferred from inclusion in the catalog.

- [Pacific Northwest Pest Management Handbooks: Aspen leaf blotch miner](https://pnwhandbooks.org/insect/hort/landscape/hosts-pests-landscape-plants/aspen-populus-tremuloides-aspen-leaf-blotch-miner)

### conyza-bonariensis

UC IPM supports rapid annual/biennial growth, disturbed agricultural settings, water competition and horseweed confusion. Common name asthmaweed is only a name, not medical advice.

- [University of California IPM: Hairy fleabane](https://ipm.ucanr.edu/weeds-identification-gallery/hairy-fleabane/)

Rejected a Flora NZ result whose Conyza heading accompanied unrelated Chasmanthe corm/orange-flower text.

- [Flora of North America: Conyza floribunda](https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=242421539)

### tamarix-aphylla

Oregon State landscape reference supports evergreen minute sheathing foliage, small flowers, drought/saline tolerance and deep roots. Avoided conflicting general naturalization statements and fire claims.

- [Oregon State University Landscape Plants: Tamarix aphylla](https://landscapeplants.oregonstate.edu/plants/tamarix-aphylla)

### hedera-hibernica

### aleuroclava-aucubae

## Review outcome and limitations

All 50 assigned species have substantive source-supported profiles; no missing-profile gap remains. Taxonomy deserves particular care for Monopterus albus, Corbicula fluminea, Conyza floribunda, Dichondra micrantha, the horticultural Asparagus densiflorus complex, and older/newer genus usage. Public summaries intentionally avoid asserted local ranges, regulatory status, medical advice, pesticide advice, and generic invasive-everywhere language. Parent must review/import these artifacts into the application; artifact completion is not a publication or release claim.
