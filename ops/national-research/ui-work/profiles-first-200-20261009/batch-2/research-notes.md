# Batch 2 research notes
Reviewed 2026-10-09. Profiles 51-100. Source URLs and labels are preserved per species in editorial.json. Notes below identify what was checked and what was deliberately left unresolved. No catalog membership was used as evidence of invasive status. No state presence/absence assessments or management recommendations were generated.

Primary evidence was read through web search and meaningful page opens. Some websites returned detailed indexed text but blocked direct opens; these are identified below. Those entries use the indexed source text, not guessed facts or URLs. All other cited sources supplied readable page or PDF content.

## Evidence by profile
- euwallacea-similis: Netherlands NPPO risk scan supports broad host range; UF/USFS Xyleborini identification resource supports slender form and rear-wing-cover characters. Older generic combinations include Xyleborus. Trap associations are not automatically confirmed breeding hosts; no specific pathogen attributed.
- syngonium-podophyllum: UF/IFAS plant directory supports juvenile leaf form, climbing habit, Florida habitat effects and garden-waste pathway. Impact language geographically bounded.
- caprella-scaura: Smithsonian NEMESIS supports attachment substrates, brooding and early maternal association. Native origin and species limits remain uncertain; no nationwide impact extrapolation.
- phellodendron-amurense: UConn plant database supports cork bark, opposite compound foliage, female fruit and Connecticut naturalization concern. No sex-independent fruit claim.
- rhinogobius-brunneus: USGS NAS indexed fact sheet supports morphology and Columbia records. Direct open failed. The source explicitly treats this as a species complex; retained that uncertainty.
- lonicera-maackii: NC State supports flower color change, opposite leaves, bird dispersal, extended leaf season and shading effects.
- lernaea-cyprinacea: UF/IFAS publication supports copepod identity, embedded adult female, egg sacs, hosts and injuries. Avoided treatment guidance.
- eichhornia-azurea: USGS NAS indexed fact sheet supports rooted habit and absence of swollen petioles; direct open failed. Did not transfer common water hyacinth impact estimates to this species. Scientific-name modernization belongs to catalog taxonomy review.
- brugmansia-suaveolens: Cambridge Botanic Garden rhythm article supports evening scent, moth pollination, toxicity and extinct-in-wild statement; botanical listing supports woody habit. Kept conservation statement attributed.
- opuntia-microdasys: NC State supports glochids, pads, flower/fruit colors and vegetative rooting. Page has potentially conflicting Arizona range wording; omitted a native-range assertion.
- dichanthium-aristatum: Tropical Forages indexed monograph supports inflorescence hairs, persistence, wet soil tolerance and vegetative growth. Direct open failed. Grazing-context dominance is not generalized to every habitat.
- sitotroga-cerealella: Canadian Grain Commission supports internal grain feeding, nonfeeding adults and lack of conspicuous webbing.
- cowpea-mild-mottle-virus: EFSA PERSEUS appendix hosted by IPPC supports symptoms, host breadth, whitefly transmission and host-dependent seed transmission. Did not repeat the old report's current-status claims or infer diagnosis from symptoms.
- avena-sterilis: CFIA seed identification supports paired shedding florets, twisted awns and seed-contaminant pathway.
- myrrhis-odorata: Dutch botanical gardens account supports fernlike scented foliage, spring emergence and geographic origin. Common name anise is ambiguous; no culinary or medicinal advice.
- rapistrum-rugosum: Texas Invasives/Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center indexed account supports rosette, fruit and Texas competition. Direct open failed. Impact statement restricted to invaded Texas habitats.
- poa-annua: UC IPM supports leaf shape, low seed-head production, cool growth and perennial populations.
- phalaris-canariensis: UBC E-Flora supports fibrous annual habit, compact inflorescence, winged glumes and disturbed habitat.
- iberis-amara: Flora of North America indexed treatment supports fruit/inflorescence features; direct open failed. Readable NC State account additionally supports fragrant ornamental flowers. No invasive designation asserted.
- vulpia-myuros: Oregon State identification page supports fine folded leaves, narrow heads and annual habit. UF/IFAS uses Festuca myuros, retained as alternate name.
- stachys-annua: Native Plant Trust key supports taproot, annual habit, pale corolla and leaf-base distinction from S. arvensis.
- mercurialis-annua: New Zealand Flora supplies diagnostic form; USDA APHIS notes broad taxonomic treatment and reproductive variation. Hesse/Pannell research article indexed methods supports its use in reproduction research; direct PMC open returned a challenge.
- polypogon-monspeliensis: UC IPM supports inflorescence, moist sites, seasonality and seed reproduction.
- lolium-multiflorum: UC IPM supports uses, rapid cool-season growth, variable longevity and hybridization with perennial ryegrass.
- diplotaxis-muralis: University of Udine indexed flora supports basal foliage, habitat and comparison with D. tenuifolia; direct open failed. Annual common name does not imply every individual dies in one season.
- melilotus-indicus: INRAE Ephytia supports tiny flowers, reticulate single-seeded pods, trifoliate leaves and agricultural habitats.
- tapinoma-litorale: Journal of Insect Science Florida Keys survey explicitly calls it native to Florida and West Indies, and lists twig/vine nesting and island records. PMC indexed article was substantive; direct open challenge. No introduced or invasive claim.
- anoecia-vagans: Blackman/Eastop A entry supports seasonal hosts and migrant distinction. Unconfirmed trapped specimens not used for US range claims.
- aphis-eugeniae: AphID species page supports host examples, geographical context and coexistence with A. spiraecola. Absence of documented vector implication is not a proof of biological impossibility.
- asiphonella-dactylonii: Blackman/Eastop A entry supports root form and Chinese host alternation; no universal migration claim.
- cerataphis-lataniae: Blackman/Eastop C entry supports wax-edged flattened form and palm association. C. brasiliensis confusion retained; older orchid records may instead concern C. orchidearum.
- dysaphis-crataegi: UC IPM separates this aphid from other carrot root/crown species. Used its specific color/wax description; avoided attributing every grouped damage statement to this species.
- eucallipterus-tiliae: UMass species account supports honeydew nuisance, leaf location, single-host cycle and established-tree tolerance. Blackman/Eastop E account reviewed independently; possible cryptic species not silently resolved.
- geoica-setulosa: USDA ARS primary research summary supports ant-associated turf observations. AphID supplies host breadth. Ant mound effects distinguished from direct aphid effects.
- geopemphigus-floccosus: Blackman/Eastop G account supports root colonies. Proposed pistache association remains unproved.
- greenidea-ficicola: Blackman/Eastop G account supports cornicles, Ficus feeding and ornamental pathway. No disease claim.
- hyalopterus-amygdali: Blackman/Eastop H account supports almond association. Reed populations and some historical records remain unresolved.
- hyperomyzus-lactucae: University of Hawaii account supports hosts, morphology and Hawaii reproduction. General vector significance retained, but avoided its potentially dated classification of individual virus-transmission modes.
- hyperomyzus-picridis: Blackman/Eastop H account supports appearance, colony position and seasonal hosts. Questionable host records excluded.
- macrosiphoniella-tanacetaria: M entry checked.
- macrosiphum-lilii: NC State author description and diagnostic photographs support morphology and lily/honeydew association. Author explicitly presumes aspects of life history; those presumptions excluded.
- melanaphis-sorini: M entry checked.
- metopolophium-festucae: M entry checked; KSU supports subspecies distinction.
- metopolophium-festucae-cerealium: KSU primary field account supports morphology, crops and Pacific Northwest context. No blanket economic-damage claim. Subspecies retained as requested queue entity.
- myzaphis-rosarum: INRAE indexed species account supports morphology, concealed feeding and limited harm. Direct opens failed twice.
- myzus-varians: INRAE Ephytia species account supports characteristic leaf rolling and peach/clematis cycle. No extrapolated calendar timing.
- pleotrichophorus-chrysanthemi: Blackman/Eastop P entry supports chrysanthemum association and unknown sexual forms. Possible conspecificity with P. glandulosus remains open.
- rhopalomyzus-lonicerae: Blackman/Eastop R entry supports contrasting seasonal forms. Snowberry cycle not asserted as confirmed.
- rhopalosiphum-rufiabdominalis: Blackman/Eastop R entry uses rufiabdominale. Queue ID preserved; supported spelling noted in prose.
- sipha-glyceriae: Blackman/Eastop S entry supports wetland hosts and cycle. No crop-loss claim.
