## Evidence standard

The four results packets retain 188 source-to-claim mappings. Sources were read directly as web pages, PDFs or explicitly labeled abstracts. Independent authorship was evaluated beyond the hostname: mirrors and summaries of the same underlying work were not treated as separate original evidence. Complementary evidence is sufficient when it supports different parts of a restrained description. Abstract-only sources support only claims visible in the abstract.

Broken or blocked pages prompted alternative retrievals. Snippets, inaccessible papers, taxonomy-only records and unverified host lists were not promoted to biological evidence. Unsupported details were removed or narrowed. Research notes identify scope limitations and source lineage.

## Important limits retained

Description readiness is separate from historical record identity. Examples include the Monopterus albus complex and Florida records now assigned to M. javanensis; historical Grateloupia doryphora records confused with G. turuturu; questioned North American Alyssum strigosum records; and older Rhinogobius brunneus records under a broader taxonomic concept. Those caveats remain in the proposed text and/or notes. No occurrence data, public species names or county determinations were changed.
