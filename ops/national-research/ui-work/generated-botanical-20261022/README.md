## Final prompts

### Botanical research
A wide landscape 3:1 botanical engraving, black ink on PURE WHITE background. Intricately detailed NATURAL botanical morphology: one gracefully arching large fern with individually articulated serrated pinnules and branching venation, oak leaves with accurate deep lobes and delicate veins, a slender branch of wildflower umbels and seedpods. These grow together across a beautiful asymmetric composition, overlap at varying depths, NOT individual icons or specimens in rows. Fine black stochastic pixel stippling and broken etched marks, no vector line geometry, no regular halftone grid. Make the fern recognizably real and exquisite, not a feather or a comb. Museum-quality naturalist drawing turned into irregular tiny pixel clusters. OPEN WHITE SPACES between leaflets and stems; airy graceful composition with strong dark detail, no huge solid flat areas. White margins on ALL four sides fading through scattered ink flecks, no clipped leaves, no frame, no words. It must look exceptionally refined and artistic, with fine individual leaf detail.

### Shared art direction for landscape and meadow
Create a premium wide panoramic editorial illustration, 3:1 aspect ratio. Style: very fine stochastic pixel engraving, intricate irregular tiny square dots and broken stippled lines, richly detailed natural morphology, organic asymmetric flowing composition dissolving at edges into transparent negative space. Absolutely no regular halftone grids, hard vector contours, smooth gradients, photos, repeated stamped flowers, basic icons, text, labels or frames. IMPORTANT OUTPUT: WHITE INK ONLY on a genuinely transparent background. All shading is expressed by sparse/dense white pixel marks separated by transparency; never solid opaque backgrounds or gray/green filled shapes. This is an alpha mask to be colored exact brand green in CSS. Keep fine details and veins visibly separated by transparent spaces. Beautiful botanical editorial art, not an identification diagram. 

Landscape subject: an imagined oblique Appalachian watershed with wooded ridges, wetland patches, a meandering stream, foreground fern and oak, and abstract cartographic fragments. No pins, labels, regular contours or hard boundaries.

Meadow subject: a richly detailed ecotone with wild bergamot, coneflower seedheads, goldenrod, prairie grasses, butterflies and distant wooded hills. Organic transitions, no geometric river stripe or stamped sunflower row.
