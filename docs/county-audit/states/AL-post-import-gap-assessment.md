# Alabama Post-Import Gap Assessment

Assessment date: `2026-06-16`

## Current Runtime Coverage

Alabama is audit complete, but it is not denominator complete.

Runtime data generated after the broad EDDMapS county snapshot refresh, ALIPC EDDMapS import, generalized Alabama Plant Atlas import, USFS Alien Forest Pest Explorer importer, USFS current invasive plant polygon importer, USDA Forest Service FIA DataMart invasive plant tables, USDA NRCS PLANTS county distribution import, NEON TALL plant presence and percent cover import, USFS Insect and Disease Survey point detection import, GBIF and iDigBio preserved specimen snapshots, GBIF eBird Observation Dataset pilot, USGS North American Breeding Bird Survey route-start pilot, Water Quality Portal biological result pilot, iNaturalist Research Grade observation pilot, APHIS federal quarantine importer, targeted NAS live collection supplement, Auburn University Formosan termite county evidence, first USFWS invasive carp eDNA non-detection records, APHIS National Honey Bee Survey Varroa sample records, and Alabama Forestry Commission Aerial Detection records shows:

- Alabama county audit progress: `67/67` counties and `14/14` chunks complete.
- Alabama live mapped species: `861` distinct species.
- Alabama average county species count: `187.19`.
- Alabama county-species matrix known determinations: `12542` verified present, `0` verified absent, `10` not detected, and `155216` unknown.
- Alabama county-species matrix known percent: `7.48%` of `167768` county-species determinations.
- Alabama category mix: still plant-heavy, but recent passes added broader insect, disease, wildlife, aquatic, specimen-backed, National Forest invasive plant, APHIS, and USFWS eDNA sampling signal.
- ALIPC denominator reconciliation: `91` list species, `78` catalog matched, `76` live mapped in Alabama, `2` catalog matched but unmapped, and `13` unmatched or ambiguous.
- Alabama ANS denominator reconciliation: `90` Appendix 12.B species, `46` catalog matched, `43` live mapped in Alabama, `3` catalog matched but unmapped, and `44` unmatched or ambiguous.
- National generated catalog: `2504` species, with `1613` mapped and `891` unmatched.

This is a major improvement from the earlier Alabama coverage gap, but it does not prove that Project Isitusa maps 90 percent or more of every invasive species present in Alabama. The attempted 10 percent knowledge milestone has not been reached because new source families still mostly add presence evidence that overlaps existing records, and public non-detection sources remain narrow or not county-importable.

## Contradictions To Preserve

- Alabama audit completion is true. Alabama denominator completion is not proven.
- Every Alabama county now has county-specific live coverage, but many counties still depend on narrow plant, aquatic, forest-pest, or disease source families rather than broad county invasive-species inventories.
- GBIF preserved specimens support verified physical occurrence by county. They do not prove establishment, invasive impact, absence in unsampled counties, or survey non-detection.
- GBIF eBird Observation Dataset rows support observed bird presence where exact species identity and coordinates or county names resolve to Alabama counties. EOD is occurrence-only and lacks sampling-event or effort metadata, so it does not support `not-detected`, absence, establishment, invasive impact, or complete county bird inventory claims. Direct EBD complete-checklist data could support cautious survey-area non-detection later, but only after an approved EBD download exists and checklist effort semantics are preserved.
- BBS route-start rows support official survey presence only where exact species identity, `RunType=1`, positive `Stop1` count, and route-start coordinates resolve to one Alabama county. They do not prove countywide presence, absence in other counties, establishment, invasive impact, non-detection, or complete bird inventory coverage. Later stops, 10-stop bins, route totals, and zero-count rows remain excluded until stop coordinates or a specific survey-area status model exists.
- iDigBio preserved specimens support verified physical occurrence by county. They overlap heavily with GBIF, IPT, Symbiota, VertNet, and collection datasets, so they should not be treated as a fully independent denominator.
- USFS Current Invasive Plant Locations supports current invasive plant infestation presence on National Forest System land in Alabama. It is not statewide plant absence evidence, and it should not be treated as complete county invasive-plant inventory coverage.
- FIA DataMart invasive subplot rows support verified-present plot observation evidence where county and exact species identity are explicit. They do not prove countywide establishment, countywide abundance, absence in counties without rows, or absence on sampled plots where the species was not recorded.
- NRCS PLANTS county distribution rows support verified-present county distribution and nativity evidence where exact species identity and county geometry are explicit. They do not prove specimen-backed occurrence, abundance, invasive impact, survey non-detection, countywide absence, or completeness for counties without rows.
- NEON TALL plant presence and percent cover rows support verified-present plot observation evidence where species identity, introduced status, and row coordinates are explicit. They do not prove countywide establishment, countywide abundance, absence in counties without rows, sampled-plot non-detection, or complete plant inventory coverage.
- WQP biological result rows support verified-present biological monitoring evidence only when `SubjectTaxonomicName` exactly matches a current catalog species, `CharacteristicName` is `Count`, the count is positive, result status is accepted or final, and point coordinates resolve to one Alabama county. Broader `characteristicName` or `characteristicType` WQP queries can return measurement rows such as fish-kill severity, tissue sex, or header-only files. Do not use those broader queries to infer species presence, non-detection, absence, or county inventory completeness.
- iNaturalist Research Grade rows support supplemental community-verified observed presence where exact active species identity and public point coordinates resolve to an Alabama county. They do not prove agency verification, establishment, invasive impact, survey non-detection, countywide absence, or completeness for counties without observations. Research Grade status can change over time, so the accepted observation IDs and access timestamp are retained in `src/data/source/inaturalist-alabama-research-grade-snapshot.json`.
- APHIS quarantine rows support active regulatory county status for reviewed exact catalog matches. They do not cover every regulated pest, and skipped rows should remain skipped unless the species exists in the catalog or a reviewed catalog addition is made.
- USFWS eDNA `No eDNA detected` samples are survey-area non-detection records, not countywide absence. Positive eDNA rows were not promoted to verified-present because the source cautions that DNA can come from live fish, dead fish, boats, birds, or water current.
- NAS live collection pages are useful for targeted county evidence, but status semantics differ by species and row. Blue Tilapia was accepted only from an established row. Koi rows were accepted as collection presence only, not establishment evidence. Failed, state-only, no-county, and no-Alabama rows were skipped.
- The Auburn University Formosan termite thesis states 16 Alabama counties, but only 9 county names were explicit in the reviewed text. Only those 9 were imported. The remaining 7 were not inferred.
- The broad EDDMapS refresh is a county presence source, not an absence or establishment source. The bounded merge used in this pass is additive for existing county unions because `src/data/source/county-presence-snapshot.json` does not store per-source county rows. Old EDDMapS-only counties cannot be safely subtracted without a full source-family rebuild.
- PestTracker, CAPS, and NAPIS remain promising survey-status lanes because their status definitions can distinguish positive and negative survey records. The public PestTracker map output reviewed on `2026-06-14` exposed Alabama county FIPS rows for some current-year pests, but those Alabama rows did not exact-match current local catalog species. All reviewed Alabama rows in that scrape were `Not Found`, so no production matrix rows were added. If imported later, `Not Found` must be `survey-area` `not-detected`, not countywide absence, and `No Survey` must remain unknown.
- APHIS National Honey Bee Survey is reputable survey data.
