# Preserved specimen metadata recovery, version 1

This opt-in pathway supports documented historical county occurrence. It does not establish current populations, abundance or complete county inventories. The owner authorized this evidence-policy improvement in the September 6, 2026 autonomous continuation instruction. Reviews are explicitly recorded as agent reviews.

A single stable occurrence ID is adequate. When that field is missing, retain the publisher core ID and namespace it by immutable archive SHA-256. Never copy an ID into another source field. Audit chosen identifiers against every archive row, including rejected records, and hold conflicting payloads before selecting a witness. Prefer a valid dated witness when several records support the same pair.

A normalized collection date may be unknown. Retain raw source fields, narrative dates and any bounds without fabricating an exact date. Reject invalid calendars, conflicting structured components and future dates. The original acquisition timestamp remains separate from new extraction and review timestamps; replay does not refresh observation freshness.

All source-specific rights, taxonomy, county and wild-occurrence requirements still apply. The pilot review caught hyphenated greenhouse cultivation and a record described as an iNaturalist observation despite its preserved-specimen label. Those records are held. Two contradictory county/locality records are also held without geographic reassignment. The exact witnesses, hashes, reasons and supporting government references are in the method review receipt. Unknown narrative dates or a label needing translation alone are not reasons to reject an otherwise adequate historical record.

The initial 191-candidate scan was unsealed and had an incorrectly attributed baseline hash. The baseline was independently reconciled to 311,667 present plus 3,244 absent pairs, then the archive was rescanned. The corrected run produced 187 candidates in 21 states. These are candidate counts until immutable runs and projections establish the integrated net gain. The 10,000-pair scale target is not a minimum acceptance threshold.
