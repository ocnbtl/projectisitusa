# Retained APHIS honey bee positive survey

Version 2.0.0 of the existing aphis-honey-bee-survey adapter adds an offline positive pathway. Version 1.0.0 and its Alabama explicit-zero pilot retain their original semantics and receipt ancestry.

The immutable August 20 acquisition retains a June 5, 2026 database export with observations through December 2025: 12,704 biological rows and one dictionary-description row. Reuse these source bytes; do not label the dictionary row a sample or acquire the CSV once per state.

A strictly positive numeric varroa_per_100_bees metric, a valid original collection month, and uniquely resolved publisher county identity support historical recorded occurrence of the sampled parasite in a composite managed-apiary sample. The source-method record pins independent NY and HI reviews, the original methods reports, publisher terms, and specific competing Hawaii evidence.

Species interpretation is program-specific: the 2016–2017 APHIS report names Varroa destructor and this alcohol-sample metric. The 2025 plan says Varroa spp. and individual CSV rows do not name a binomial. Retain this distinction in every assertion. Other parasite, virus and pesticide columns are outside this method.

The source pools samples from eight managed colonies per apiary. It includes migratory and stationary operations without disclosing individual migration history. Detection does not establish wild populations, countywide prevalence, permanent residence, local acquisition or persistence today. Managed honey bees do not by themselves show that their parasites were intentionally captive.

Resolve only published state and county text through the pinned active registry. The publisher used 2020 TIGER/Line boundaries. Former Connecticut counties, retired Alaska names, unknown names and ambiguous county/city names cannot be assigned automatically.

Kauai 15007 and Maui 15009 remain held under v1 because five positive rows conflict with the University of Hawaii summary of the same 2009–2018 survey. Preserve both sides. No contamination, coding error or transient-incursion explanation has been established. The HDOA/HISC not-known-established zeros do not create countywide absence.

Each selected county produces one aggregate assertion with its latest positive YYYY-MM, all positive row identities, and a witness artifact containing every selected county row, including zeros and unavailable metrics. Grouped rejection records explain rows excluded from the positive claim. Anonymous rows use an immutable archive hash, parsed row index, physical line locator and full original line hash excluding its terminator. Identical coarse fields cannot establish duplicate sampling events; no public apiary ID is invented.

The complete selected snapshot scan supports a completed source outcome only. It does not complete the species protocol, certify the county, or turn zero/blank/rejected data into non-detection or absence. No upstream requests occur during replay.

Retain University of Maryland ownership, copyright and non-endorsement attribution. Its terms permit scientific and educational reproduction; do not label the source CC0. Cite the stable public download page and original retrieval date, not an expiring signed object URL.

MAIN commits the method and exact state plans, dispatches isolated frozen leases, reviews canonical reconstruction and worker lineage, integrates accepted immutable runs, and compiles once for the national batch. Count exact new county-species determinations only after projection reconciliation. R2 publication, production release and local research gains remain separate checkpoints.
