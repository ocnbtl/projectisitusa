# ISITUSA brand artwork

The brand is ISITUSA, one word in uppercase. The full name remains Invasive Species In The United States of America.

The seven approved v4 outlines are preserved, with the extra space after T removed. SVGs have no font dependencies. Green, white, and black wordmarks include transparent PNG companions; the emblem-and-name lockup includes SVG and PNG.

Keep the artwork proportional. Rebuild with node scripts/build-brand-v5.mjs. The v3 emblem remains the favicon. Older versioned kits are historical artifacts, not the current identity.
