# ISITUSA botanical identity

The acronym is ISITUSA, with no space. Wordmark, full name and editorial headings share ISITUSA Field Display. Full-name capitalization is `Invasive Species In The United States of America`.

The pre-existing circular emblem is preserved. These SVGs contain outlined letters; they do not require an installed font. Green artwork is for light backgrounds; white is for dark backgrounds. Preserve the aspect ratio. PNGs are derived from the SVGs with embedded provenance.

The type direction takes inspiration from botanical curves and sharp leaf-like terminals. No NYBG artwork or font files are used. See `/fonts/isitusa-display/README.md` and `OFL.txt` for the openly licensed font basis and modifications.
