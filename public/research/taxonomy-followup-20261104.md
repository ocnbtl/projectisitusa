# Follow-up taxonomy review: 4 November 2026

## Scope and result

The current 2,504-entry taxonomy file was checked against the earlier 24-case audit. That audit had resolved 23 identities, with two incomplete higher classifications. Verticillium albo-atrum remains the only unresolved identity; it is not 24 still-open cases. A scan of the other matched classification notes found no additional explicit uncertainty flags. This is a follow-up to the flagged cases, not a new independent verification of all 2,504 identities.

## Eucalyptus leaf beetle: classification expanded

Catalog ID: chrysophtharta-m-fuscum. EPPO's [CPTHMF entry](https://gd.eppo.int/taxon/CPTHMF) explicitly connects Chrysophtharta m-fuscum with Paropsisterna m-fuscum and supplies Animalia, Arthropoda, Hexapoda and Insecta, which were missing from our entry. Those four ranks are now added. The previously verified order, family, genus, species and subfamily remain. The subfamily is supported by [Reid & de Little (2013)](https://www.biotaxa.org/Zootaxa/article/download/zootaxa.3681.4.4/53840). The synonym flag now agrees with the explicit older-name relationship. No species were merged and no occurrence was reassigned.

## Psammonobiotus dziwnowi: identity verified, deeper placement still open

Catalog ID: psammonobiotus-dziwnowi. [Nicholls (2005), pages 271-278](https://rcin.org.pl/Content/266/PDF/WA488_3835_P1825-T44-nr3_AP.pdf#page=80), is the original species description and supports the author/year and Psammonobiotidae family. [USGS / NOAA GLANSIS](https://nas.er.usgs.gov/queries/greatLakes/FactSheet.aspx?Species_ID=2654) supports the species and family independently.

[Adl et al. (2019), page 70](https://amoeba.msstate.edu/pdfs/Adl_etal.2018.RevisedClassification.JEMB.pdf#page=67), places Psammonobiotidae within Cercozoa with unresolved closer placement. Euglyphida is suggested, not established. The paper uses a hierarchy without formal rank designations. We therefore explain Cercozoa in the note without forcing it into a phylum/class/order field or mixing its scheme with the older Rhizopoda/Filosea framework. The family, genus and species stay verified; the partial-hierarchy status stays visible in the data and the uncertainty is explained on the profile.

What would resolve the remaining gap: a reliable taxonomic treatment establishing the family's closer placement. A naming preference or user approval cannot replace that evidence.
